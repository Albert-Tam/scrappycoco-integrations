# Deliverable criteria

Choose the relevant playbook; these are not stages every task must pass through.

## Positioning and launch

Combine verified product facts, audience problems in their own words, competing
alternatives and the user's differentiator. Produce a positioning statement,
message hierarchy, proof points and claims needing validation. A launch plan
includes audience, offer, channels, concrete assets, owners, dependencies and
measures. Draft the requested assets. Separate known dates/budgets from assumptions.

## Creator campaign

For a broad UGC campaign, show the research sequence before creating the brief.
Example after verifying the relevant catalog capabilities (otherwise mark the
specific steps conditional):

> For Qolaig, I'll use Coco's research tools to:
> 1. **Find creators.** Shortlist five French-speaking Instagram/TikTok creators
>    with relevant software or workflow demonstrations and portfolio links.
> 2. **Research hooks.** Review five recent Reels/TikTok openings and relevant
>    conversations on X for audience problems, with sources and dates.
> 3. **Build the creative.** Use those findings to draft three original workflow
>    scripts and match them to suitable creators.
>
> I recommend this sequence so the casting and scripts draw on real examples.
> Let's continue?

Adapt platforms, counts and language rather than treating this as a fixed recipe.
Inspect creator, hook and trend capabilities plus relevant native platform tools;
use the capability guidance for lookup hints. Recent or popular posts alone do
not establish a viral trend. Distinguish hired presenters from influencer
partnerships: portfolio fit matters for production; audience evidence matters
when purchasing distribution. Do not promise that five qualified creators exist.
On acceptance, show the strongest candidates and sourced hook examples in chat,
including gaps, before developing the full campaign package. Explicit delegation
or a previously accepted plan overrides the example's approval question.


Return a deduplicated shortlist with profile identity, niche/platform/region fit,
recent relevant post URLs, observed metrics and dates, public contact provenance,
partnership evidence and missing criteria. Match each creator to a specific
campaign idea. Draft a creator brief and, when requested, personalized outreach
with deliverables, creative freedom, disclosure needs and usage-rights questions.
Do not promise availability, commercial terms or send/contract on research authority.

## Short-form content and paid creative

For every concept, deliver the opening words and visual, narrative beats,
proof/demo, CTA, placement/length, production notes and the test hypothesis.
Keep observed hooks separate from original scripts and specify source evidence.
An ad package includes primary text, headline and variations when applicable.
A content calendar includes actual post concepts/copy, format, audience and CTA;
mark dates as proposed until scheduled. Do not label untested drafts winners.

## LinkedIn content and publishing

Use [LinkedIn and demand](linkedin-demand.md) for profile positioning, editorial
strategy, original posts, calendars, public comments/replies and performance review.
Deliver finished platform-appropriate copy with supported claims, available assets
and a clear next step. If a demo recording would help but is unavailable, finish a
usable text version and mark recording as optional unless the requested format
requires it. Publish or schedule only through a verified client tool within the
user's mandate, and preserve the confirmed post URL/status. Hand qualified prospect
follow-up to VP of Sales; keep public engagement and posting with the CMO.

## Organic distribution and communities

Find relevant conversations and read their context before drafting. Verify
community promotion rules separately; inaccessible rules are an explicit gap.
Make contributions useful in context rather than generic product mentions.
Prepare actual posts/replies where requested. Posting and DMs remain distinct
actions requiring the relevant mandate and connection.

## B2B demand and sales partnership

Translate the agreed ICP into explicit filters, gather a bounded account sample,
verify fit and build a campaign proposition for each segment. Company enrichment
supports targeting; workforce and job signals are hypotheses to investigate.
Hand Sales the company identities, buyer-role evidence, message/offer, exclusion
criteria and receipts. Define a qualified handoff with the user rather than
optimizing for raw lead volume. Do not silently turn marketing into mass outreach.

## Conversion and retention

Use the supplied funnel, landing page or customer evidence to identify friction.
Draft the requested page/email/onboarding changes and explain the hypothesis.
Public competitor material can inspire tests but cannot establish the user's
conversion rates. Account access, customer messaging and production changes need
verified tools and the relevant mandate. Finish with testable assets and a
measurement plan, not unsupported forecasts.
