# Sales playbooks

Read the workflow relevant to the current bottleneck. Adapt it to the user's
business and goal; do not execute every playbook by default.

For practical methods and original examples, use [sales strategy](sales-strategy.md)
and [outbound and pipeline](outbound-pipeline.md). Strategy covers focus, deals
and weekly direction; outbound covers qualified pipeline, messaging and replies.
Read only what the current deliverable needs. Hand LinkedIn posting and public
engagement to the CMO with the existing brief and evidence.

When the user selects multiple channels, prepare a bounded, usable package for
each. Use these criteria before claiming readiness; do not count a strategy note
or generic template as completed outreach. Keep partial work labeled and continue
resolvable research gaps within the mandate.

| Channel | Ready for the user's review |
| --- | --- |
| Community replies outside LinkedIn | Direct discussion links, dates/recent activity, evidence of product fit, checked community promotion rules, and replies tailored to the actual question |
| Cold outreach | Verified companies and relevant named buyers where obtainable, source links/dates, fit and need evidence, a usable contact route or explicit reachability gap, and recipient-specific drafts |

## Communities: respond to an actual need

For Reddit, follow [research capabilities](research-capabilities.md): inspect
native search/feed/comments before generic extraction. If unconfigured, record
that environment limitation and use a supported fallback. Check recency with
inspected filters; a generic web login wall is not a Reddit-provider test.

Read the discussion and relevant replies, not just a search snippet. Preserve the
URL, post date, latest relevant activity and the need the product can address.
Distinguish an active request from an older relevant example; an old post alone
does not establish current intent or justify switching to a direct message.

Check accessible community rules for promotion, disclosure and contact. Record
the rule source; if rules are inaccessible or unclear, mark that review gap
instead of presenting the reply as ready to post. Draft a useful answer to the
question and mention the product only when relevant and allowed. State the
sender's actual affiliation; do not assume they are the founder. Sending or
publishing still needs the user's mandate.

## Focus: ICP and the first experiment

Use the product, actual customer examples and wins/losses when supplied. Separate
the company that could use the product from the buyer who can sponsor it. Define
must-have fit, disqualifiers, a plausible pain, and observable buying signals.
A hiring post or funding event is a signal to investigate, not proof of intent.

Deliver an ICP hypothesis with evidence, the proposed offer, one reachable segment,
and an experiment with a success metric and timebox. Label hypotheses. If there
is no track record, do not invent conversion rates or present a revenue forecast
as a fact. Recommend the smallest evidence-gathering action that would resolve
the biggest uncertainty.

## Pipeline: account research and qualification

Before acquisition, define what qualifies an account, requested fields, exclusions
and the evidence required. Inspect live research tools and apply the operational
Scrappycoco workflow and [B2B database guidance](research-capabilities.md).
Inspect relevant company/employee/job capabilities before defaulting to a
directory-only list; use them when they fit and are configured. For unvalidated repeated-template batches, follow its
representative test and scale-up gate; do not replace it with a sequence of blind
single-provider retries.

Deliver a table or CSV with company, domain, segment/geography, fit status,
supporting source URLs and dates, buying signal if evidenced, relevant buyer role,
public business contact if actually found, missing fields, and a next action.
Use qualified / disqualified / needs review, with reasons tied to the user's
rubric. You, the calling client, judge sales fit. Do not imply Scrappycoco ranked
the accounts or chose a provider for their semantic quality.

Deduplicate by stable company identity/domain. Mark unknowns explicitly. Never
guess an email from a person's name or label an unverified address deliverable.
Reconcile requested and captured accounts, duplicates, rejected matches, unresolved
gaps and truncation. Keep raw acquisition output alongside the usable table.
Do not inflate results to hit a target count.

Next action examples: resolve the buyer role for the strongest matches; draft a
message using a verified signal; refine an ICP criterion that rejected most leads.

## Outreach: messages worth sending

Use company context, buyer role, evidenced pain and approved claims. Prepare
subject, opening, concise offer and a clear CTA. Personalization must be traceable
to a source. A hypothesis can become a question, not a claim about the prospect.
Provide a follow-up sequence only when useful; avoid repeating the same pitch.

Keep a recipient/message mapping, source links, channel and draft status. Sending
needs an available tool and explicit authorization covering recipients, content
and scope. A user's approved campaign may authorize multiple sends; do not force
a new approval for each item within that scope. Honor opt-outs and exclusions.
Log tool-confirmed delivery state; inspect an ambiguous failure before retrying.

Next action examples: prepare the first five personalized drafts for review;
compare actual reply objections; change the offer after evidence of poor fit.

## Deals: advance the buyer's decision

Use a supplied pipeline export, notes, or an available CRM read connection. Ask
for missing fields only when needed: deal amount/currency, stage, expected close,
last meaningful activity, buyer problem, stakeholders and agreed next step.

Deliver a deal-by-deal view of observed facts, unknowns, stall/risk reasons,
discovery questions, and the next mutually useful action with an owner/date.
Do not fabricate a champion, budget, urgency or a buyer commitment. Draft a
meeting brief or follow-up when it helps unblock the next action.

Show pipeline totals separately by currency; deduplicate opportunity IDs and use
the user's stage definitions. Weighted forecasts require supplied probabilities
or visibly labeled scenarios. Do not turn stage labels into invented certainty.
CRM writebacks require the user's authorization for the exact update scope.

## Weekly direction: what changed and what matters next

Compare a dated baseline with actual observed outcomes. State the period and
definitions. Distinguish accounts researched, messages sent, replies, qualified
meetings, opportunities created, pipeline value and closed revenue.

Calculate conversion rates only with a valid denominator and comparable cohort.
If a denominator is zero/missing, report unavailable. Explain stale or incomplete
data. Never use a sample rate as a forecast without stating the assumption and
uncertainty. When comparing currencies, keep them separate unless an explicit
conversion rate and date are available.

Deliver a short sales brief: goal progress, bottleneck evidence, the highest-value
next actions with owners, and one decision needed from the user, if any. Save it
with supporting files. A recurring brief requires the client's verified scheduler
and the user's cadence; writing a plan does not create a scheduled job.
